The dataset is used unmodified in substance: Vertex converts the CSV ranges
into compact binary tables (Resources/GeoIP/country-v4.bin.z and
Resources/GeoIP/country-v6.bin.z) that map IPv4 and IPv6 address ranges to
ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country codes. The conversion is done by
Scripts/build-geoip.sh and Scripts/geoip-encode.py in the Vertex source tree.

No attribution is displayed per lookup; the attribution is provided here, in
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and in the app's open-source licenses screen.

The "Lite" database is offered by DB-IP free of charge and is less accurate
than their commercial products. Vertex uses it only to pick the flag shown
next to a connected peer; it is never used for access control or billing.

DB-IP makes no warranty of any kind, express or implied, about the accuracy or
completeness of the data.
